Test (in `scripts/variants.ts`): against the piece's default moveset, the variant must move the 1v1 rating by ≥ 150 *and* across 500 **in its favour**, in at least 2 of s11 / s22 / s00 / ch, against at least 2 meaningful opponents; and it may not lose more such matchups than it gains.
